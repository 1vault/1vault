# Introduction

**1Vault** - Capital in Motion.

Strategist and investor park SOL into the **same vault**. The strategist signs. The vault pays. Close pays leftover SOL **by share weight**, not 50/50.

## Product in one screen

- **Shared book** - one vault for everyone who parks  
- **Strategist** - creates the vault, locks $1VAULTS, parks, signs trades, closes  
- **Investor** - parks into a public vault, rides the book, can withdraw  
- **Park / Sign / Ride / Close** - the verbs that matter  
- **$1VAULTS licence** - lock **1,000,000 $1VAULTS** to create; returned **in full** on close  
- **Non-custodial** - vault funds stay on-chain; your keys stay in your browser
