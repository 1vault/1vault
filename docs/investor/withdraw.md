# Withdraw

Redeem your vault **shares** back toward wallet SOL. On the current product, withdraw is **free** (no platform withdraw fee) - you still pay network fees.

## When to withdraw

- You want out before the strategist closes  
- You are reducing size  
- The vault is still **Active** and allows redeem  

---

## From Park → My parks

1. Open [Park](/extension/park-and-capital) for the vault (or via detail)  
2. Open the **My parks** tab  
3. Find your park row  
4. Tap **Withdraw**  
5. Approve signatures (**Withdrawing…**)  
6. Success toast: **Withdrawn** (or similar)  
7. Holdings / wallet SOL refresh after confirm  

Empty: “No parks yet — park SOL from the Park tab.”

## From Home holdings

Home → **Holdings** may also expose **Withdraw** on your investor parks when listed.

---

## Close vs withdraw

| Path | Who | Result |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Withdraw shares** | Any shareholder | Exit your weight early (while vault allows) |
| **Close vault** | Strategist | Whole book winds down; everyone paid by **share weight**; strategist unlocks $1VAULTS |

If the strategist closes, you receive close payout by weight - you may not need a separate withdraw.

---

## Failures

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Insufficient shares | Lower amount |
| Vault closed / closing | Use close settlement; new parks blocked |
| Liquidity tight | Wait for exits / value update, retry |
| Wallet locked | Unlock first |
