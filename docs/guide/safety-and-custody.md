# Safety & custody

Read this before parking size you cannot afford to lose.

## Non-custodial model

- Vault funds stay in on-chain vault accounts - not a 1Vault hot wallet that can walk away with deposits  
- Your extension key stays in an **encrypted keyring** on your device - you approve every send  
- Never paste your seed or password into random sites or chats  

## Risks that remain

| Risk | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Trading / market** | Shared book can lose value. Memecoins are volatile. |
| **Smart contract** | Bugs and upgrades can affect funds. |
| **UX / phishing** | Wrong vault, fake extensions, social engineering. |
| **Keyring** | Weak password, malware, or unlocked session on a shared machine. |
| **Liquidity / close** | Positions must be flat before close; exits have slippage. |

## What 1Vault does **not** promise

- No guaranteed returns  
- No “safe yield”  
- No 50/50 close with the strategist  
- No insurance fund in these docs  

## Good hygiene

1. Use a **dedicated** trading wallet - not your cold savings  
2. Lock the keyring when idle  
3. Confirm vault and trader before you park  
4. Exit positions before Close; understand [share-weight payout](/guide/concepts#close-by-share-weight)  
5. Install only from the official download when it is published  

## Licence tokens

$1VAULTS locked at create returns on close - only after a successful close. Locked $1VAULTS is not trading inventory.
